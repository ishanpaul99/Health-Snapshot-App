About Health Snapshot

Health Snapshot is an educational health calculator that calculates BMI, estimates daily calorie requirements for maintenance and weight goals, calculates healthy weight ranges for your height, and provides educational health guidance based on standard formulas.

It is intended for informational purposes only and is not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making changes to your diet or exercise routine.
